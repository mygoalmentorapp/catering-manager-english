# Opening Screen Design (from dark package)

## Visual Elements:
- Dark background (#0A1628 to #0D1B2A gradient)
- Chef hat/cloche icon at top with decorative lines
- Diamond separator ornament
- Title: "קיבלת גישה מוקדמת" (bold, white + gold accent on "גישה")
- Subtitle paragraph explaining the app
- Card with "מה אפשר לעשות עכשיו?" header
  - 4 rows with icons and checkmarks: products, orders, shopping lists, feedback
- Two buttons at bottom:
  - "המשך לאפליקציה" - teal/accent filled button with glow
  - "תן משוב" - orange/gold outlined button
- Footer text: "תודה שאתה חלק מהשלב הראשון של האפליקציה."
- Diamond ornament at very bottom

## Colors:
- Background: dark navy (#0A1628 / #0D1B2A)
- Card: dark with teal border glow
- Primary button: teal (#3AAFA9) filled
- Secondary button: orange/gold (#D4A574) outlined
- Title accent: gold (#D4A574)
- Text: white/light gray
- Icons: teal and gold
- Checkmarks: teal circles
